Cloak by Lawgistics
For Malaysian disputes firms

Use AI on disputes work without handing over your client's details.

Cloak hides client details in a folder of documents before they go to an AI tool, keeps the key encrypted on your own drive, and puts the real details back into the AI's draft. Everything runs on your own computers.

1. Your documentsWord, PDF, scans, Excel, emails. Only ever read.
2. Cloak a folderNames and numbers become tags like {{PERSON_1}}
3. ReviewA named person decides every uncertain word.
4. Cloaked copiesChecked against the key, then saved to a folder you choose.
Your AI toolChosen and used by your lawyer. Sees tags, not names.
5. Restore a draftReal details back in, and the draft checked.

Steps 1 to 5 run on your computer and make no network connections. Only the cloaked copies your lawyer chooses go to the AI company. Cloak itself sends nothing.

What it hides

People, companies, NRIC and passport numbers, addresses, phone numbers, emails, bank accounts, case numbers, land titles and vehicle plates. The same person gets the same tag in every file and every spelling.

Strict mode (the default) also blurs dates, amounts, reference numbers, listed places, project names, some job titles and court names.

Every part of every file, or the file is blocked

  • Word headers, footers, comments and tracked changes (deleted text too); hidden Excel sheets; email attachments; file and folder names
  • Scanned pages through OCR on your machine: English, Malay, Chinese, Tamil
  • A file it cannot read completely is listed with the reason, never exported

Built for partners and IT to check

  • Offline. No internet needed. IT can block it in the firewall and it still works.
  • Encrypted key. AES-256-GCM, opened by a password or a printed recovery code. No back door.
  • Compulsory review. A named person decides every item, and any real value left in the output stops the export.
  • Log. Who did what, and when. Never a real name or number.
  • Checked drafts. Flags tags the AI invented and figures not found in your documents.

You still decide

Which AI tool to use, and its terms on keeping data and training. Whether a matter suits AI at all. And your lawyer reads every export before it leaves the firm.

Cloak lowers the risk; it does not remove it.

It uses rules and word lists, not an AI model, and it misses things. On a made-up matter of 22 files it had never seen, names were still visible in 13 files after cloaking (nicknames, single names, lowercase names in file notes). Several gaps have been fixed since. So a person reviews every export, and a second, independent check is being built. The AI company can still see what kind of dispute it is.
