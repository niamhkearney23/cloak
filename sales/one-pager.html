Cloak by Lawgistics
For Malaysian litigation and dispute resolution firms

Hide your client's details before documents go to an AI tool.

Cloak hides client details in a folder of documents before they go to an AI tool, keeps the key encrypted on your own drive, and puts the real details back into the AI's draft. Cloak runs entirely on your own computers.

1. Your documentsWord, PDF, scans, Excel, emails (.eml). Only ever read.
2. Cloak a folderNames and numbers become tags like {{PERSON_1}}
3. ReviewA named person decides every uncertain word.
4. Cloaked copiesChecked a second time, then saved to a folder you choose.
Your AI toolChosen and used by your lawyer. Sees tags in place of the names Cloak hid.
5. Restore a draftReal details back in, and the draft checked.

Steps 1 to 5 run on your computer. Cloak has no network code and its window refuses web requests (tested on Linux; IT can confirm on Windows and Mac). Only the cloaked copies your lawyer chooses go to the AI company. Cloak itself sends nothing.

What it hides

People, companies, NRIC and passport numbers, addresses, phone numbers, emails, bank accounts, case numbers, land titles and vehicle plates. The same person gets the same tag in every file, including the other spellings and short forms it can link.

Strict mode (the default) also blurs dates, amounts, reference numbers, listed places, project names, some job titles and court names.

Every part of every file, or the file is blocked (with exceptions)

  • Word headers, footers, comments and tracked changes (deleted text too); hidden Excel sheets; email attachments; file and folder names
  • Scanned pages through OCR on your machine: English and Malay. A Chinese or Tamil page is held for a person to check
  • A file with a part it cannot read is listed and never exported; a page it cannot read reliably is held until a named person approves it
  • Not read, not blocking: routing headers, stamps, barcodes, handwriting, small text in pictures

Built for partners and IT to check

  • Offline. No internet needed. IT can block it in the firewall and it still works.
  • Encrypted key. AES-256-GCM, opened by a password or a printed recovery code (a person can let Windows or macOS remember the password for their login). No back door.
  • Compulsory review. A named person decides every uncertain word. Then a double check must pass: each key value, ID number or possible name it still finds is hidden or marked as fine by a named person before anything is exported.
  • Log. Who did what, and when. Never a name or number from the documents (it names the lawyers who used Cloak).
  • Checked drafts. Flags tags the AI invented and, when you point it at your documents, figures not found in them.

You still decide

Which AI tool to use, and its terms on keeping data and training. Whether a matter suits AI at all. And your lawyer reads each export before it leaves the firm.

Cloak lowers the risk; it does not remove it.

It uses rules and word lists, not an AI model, and it misses things. On a 22-file made-up matter written outside our rule-writing team (run once on 12 November 2026, an earlier version, counted by hand), names were still visible in 13 files, and the client's name in all 22. New name rules, measured on a separate 15-file made-up matter written by our own team, cut silent misses from 59 to 6 of 278 name places. Small tests, not miss rates. So a person reviews every export and the double checker looks again first. The AI company can still see what kind of dispute it is.
