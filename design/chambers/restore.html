Payment Response

under section 6 of the Construction Industry Payment and Adjudication Act 2012

1We act for Teratai Kenanga Development Sdn Bhd (“our client”), the employer under the construction agreement dated 17 February 2023 (“the Agreement”) with Bina Jaya Construction Sdn Bhd (“the Claimant”).

2We refer to the Claimant’s payment claim dated 12 March 2024 for RM 1,284,600.00 for additional works at Block C (“the Payment Claim”).

3Our client disputes the Payment Claim in full, for the reasons set out below.

4The additional works were never instructed in writing by our client’s representative, Mohd Faizal bin Ismail, as clause 4.2 of the Agreement requires.

5The Claimant’s own email of 12 March 2024, sent by Lee Chee Keong, puts the same claim at RM 1,248,600.00. The two figures cannot both be right.

6The valuation by Priya a/p Raghavan has not been served on our client, and no sum can be due on it.

7Our client has in any event paid RM 1,250,000.00 on account, which the Claimant has not credited.

8The performance bond of RM 922,500.00 remains in force and is not affected by this response.

9The minutes of site meeting 23 record a delay of 46 days between 12 March 2024 and 23 April 2024, for which the Claimant alone is responsible.

10Our client reserves its right to claim liquidated damages under clause 22 of the Agreement.

11Nothing in this Payment Response is an admission that any sum is due to the Claimant.

12All correspondence should be sent to our client’s site office at Kuala Kubu Bharu, marked for the attention of Mohd Faizal bin Ismail.
